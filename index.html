College of Computing and Information Sciences

Educates computer scientists, IT professionals and information systems specialists who build software, networks and digital services.

  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Information Systems

College of Teacher Education

Prepares elementary teachers and secondary teachers specializing in Mathematics, Science and English for the schools of Antique and Western Visayas.

  • Bachelor of Secondary Education
    • Major in Mathematics
    • Major in Science
    • Major in English
  • Bachelor of Elementary Education

tlmc.cte@antiquespride.edu.ph

College of Business and Management

Develops hospitality professionals, entrepreneurs and local government administrators through programs in hospitality management, entrepreneurship and local governance.

  • Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management
  • Bachelor of Science in Entrepreneurship
  • Bachelor of Local Government and Administration

tlmc.cbm@antiquespride.edu.ph

College of Fisheries

Trains fisheries professionals in aquaculture, capture fisheries, post-harvest technology and coastal resource management through the Bachelor of Science in Fisheries.

  • Bachelor of Science in Fisheries
