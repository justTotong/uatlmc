University Seal

University of Antique

The University Seal

The university seal has the following symbolism as approved by the Board of Regents by virtue of BOR Resolution No. 96, series of 2010.

Birds in Flight

The four flying birds symbolize the four core values of the university which are excellence, commitment, integrity, social responsibility as well as its four thrusts which are instruction, research, extension, and resource generation.

The Flora

The plant signifies agriculture which is the major livelihood of the province.
Water: The water represents the body of water in the shores of Antique and signifies aquaculture as another major livelihood of Antiqueños.

Light

The light represents studies, research, truth, and education.

Triangle

The triangle represents the stability of the University amidst the challenges of change and time.

The University Colors

The official colors of the University shall be Red and Gold. The colors represent the four core values of the University. Red stands for integrity and commitment while gold represents excellence and social responsibility.

Red: integrity and commitment Gold: excellence and social responsibility
