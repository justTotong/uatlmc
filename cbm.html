College of Business and Management

University of Antique Tario-Lim Memorial Campus

The College of Business and Management develops hospitality professionals, entrepreneurs and local government administrators, drawing on the tourism of Tibiao and the businesses and local governments of northern Antique.

  • On-the-job training in resorts, hotels and restaurants in Antique and nearby provinces
