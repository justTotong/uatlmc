How to apply

Send the following documents to tlmc@antiquespride.edu.ph or submit them to the Human Resource Management Office on or before the deadline:

  1. Letter of intent addressed to the University President, stating the position and item number applied for
  2. Fully accomplished Personal Data Sheet (CS Form No. 212, Revised 2017) with recent passport-sized photo
  3. Work Experience Sheet
  4. Photocopy of certificate of eligibility, rating or license
  5. Photocopy of transcript of records and diploma
  6. Photocopy of certificates of relevant trainings
  7. Performance rating in the last rating period, if applicable

Incomplete applications will not be processed.

Selection process

  1. SubmissionApplicants send complete documents before the deadline.
  2. Initial screeningApplications are checked against the qualification standards.
  3. AssessmentQualified applicants take the examination, demonstration teaching or interview.
  4. DeliberationThe Human Resource Merit Promotion and Selection Board evaluates the applicants.
  5. AppointmentThe appointing authority selects and appoints the applicant.

Equal opportunity

The University of Antique adheres to the Equal Employment Opportunity Principle. There is no discrimination in the selection of employees on account of age, sex, sexual orientation and gender identity, civil status, disability, religion, ethnicity, or political affiliation.
